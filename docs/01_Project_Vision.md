# SportsHub

## 1. Introduction

SportsHub is a centralized sports tournament management platform designed for educational institutions. It aims to digitize the organization and management of university sports tournaments by providing real-time match updates, tournament management, player statistics, fixtures, and historical records.

Traditional university sports events often rely on WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, or manual announcements to communicate schedules and results. SportsHub replaces these fragmented communication channels with a unified platform where students, coordinators, and administrators can efficiently manage and follow sporting events.

The initial version of SportsHub will focus on cricket tournaments conducted within a university, while the overall architecture will be designed to support multiple sports and multiple universities in the future.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

University sports tournaments are an important part of student life, but managing and following these events is often difficult due to the absence of a centralized digital platform.

During tournaments such as **Khelo MBM**, multiple sports are conducted simultaneously at different venues across the campus. Students who are interested in following these events cannot physically be present at every ground, making it difficult to stay updated with ongoing matches.

Currently, tournament schedules, fixture updates, and announcements are mostly shared through WhatsApp groups. If a match is postponed or rescheduled, there is no single reliable source where students can verify the latest information.

For sports like cricket, organizers sometimes rely on third-party applications such as CricHeroes for live scoring. However, these applications are designed primarily for cricket and do not provide a unified platform for managing other sports, tournaments, player records, or university-specific workflows.

Additionally, once a tournament concludes, there is very little historical information available regarding player performances, match statistics, tournament winners, or team achievements. Valuable sports records are often lost after the event.

As a result, students, players, and organizers lack a single platform that can:

- Display live scores of ongoing matches.
- Provide tournament schedules and fixture updates.
- Maintain player statistics and achievements.
- Store historical tournament records.
- Manage university sports digitally.

SportsHub aims to solve these problems by creating a centralized sports ecosystem for educational institutions.

---

# 3. Vision

Our vision is to build SportsHub into a comprehensive digital platform that transforms how universities organize and experience sports tournaments.

Rather than being limited to displaying live scores, SportsHub aims to become the official digital sports ecosystem for educational institutions by providing tournament management, player databases, real-time scoring, performance analytics, historical records, and communication tools.

In the long term, the platform should support multiple universities, multiple sports, mobile applications, advanced analytics, and intelligent features while remaining simple for organizers and engaging for students.

---

# 4. Objectives

The primary objectives of SportsHub are:

- Digitize university sports tournament management.
- Provide real-time live scores for ongoing matches.
- Create a centralized platform for all sports conducted within a university.
- Maintain detailed player profiles and statistics.
- Display tournament fixtures and schedules.
- Instantly notify students about schedule changes.
- Store complete historical records of tournaments.
- Reduce dependency on WhatsApp groups and third-party applications.
- Enable role-based management for university administrators and sports coordinators.
- Design a scalable architecture capable of supporting multiple universities in the future.

# 5. Target Users

## 5.1 Student

### Needs

- View live scores.
- View upcoming fixtures.
- Check tournament schedules.
- View player statistics.
- Access completed match results.
- Follow tournament standings.

### Permissions

- View-only access.
- Cannot modify any data.

---

## 5.2 Sports Coordinator

### Needs

- Create tournaments.
- Create fixtures.
- Register teams and players.
- Manage live scoring.
- Update schedules.
- Publish match results.

### Permissions

- Full control over the sports assigned to them.
- Cannot manage sports assigned to other coordinators.

---

## 5.3 University Administrator

### Needs

- Manage the entire platform.
- Assign sports coordinators.
- Create and manage sports.
- Manage user permissions.
- Monitor tournaments.
- Configure university-level settings.

### Permissions

- Full administrative access.
- Can assign or revoke coordinator roles.
- Can manage all tournaments and sports.

---

# 6. Future Vision

The initial release (Version 1) will focus on supporting cricket tournaments within a single university.

Future versions of SportsHub may include:

- Support for multiple sports (Football, Kabaddi, Volleyball, Basketball, etc.).
- Multi-university architecture.
- Dedicated Android and iOS applications.
- AI-generated match summaries.
- Push notifications.
- Live streaming integration.
- Online player registration and trial management.
- Performance-based player selection.
- Coach and referee portals.
- Advanced analytics dashboards.
- Public player profiles and achievements.
- Digital certificates and tournament awards.
